# Soccer_Ball
FIFA 21 Player Analysis — Key Findings
1. Top Rated Players
The highest rated player in FIFA 21 is L. Messi with OVA 93, followed by Cristiano Ronaldo (92) and R. Lewandowski (91) / Neymar Jr (91).
Insight: Most top-rated players are forwards / attackers.

2. Nationality Distribution
England has the most players in FIFA 21 with ~1,700 players, followed by Germany and Spain.
Insight: This reflects the football popularity in European regions.

3. Highest Paying Clubs
Real Madrid (or FC Barcelona) pays the highest average weekly wage at ~€200,000+, with all top 10 clubs coming from Europe's Big Five leagues.
Insight: Football wealth is concentrated in just 5 European nations (England, Spain, Italy, Germany, and France).

4. Age vs Overall Rating
The relationship follows an inverted U-shaped (quadratic) curve, with ratings peaking around age 27–29 before declining.
Insight: Player prime years appear to be between 26 and 30.

5. Market Value vs Rating
Market value increases exponentially with OVA.
A player rated 90 is worth roughly 3x to 5x more than a player rated 80.
Insight: The football market values elite talent disproportionately.
